Welcome to Flicker @ UJ

Campus life shouldn't blur into deadlines and stress. Flicker @ UJ brings joy through small, meaningful moments — from silent discos under the stars to poetry nights that connect hearts.

Our mission is to create safe, inclusive, and affordable experiences that nurture student well-being and connection.

About Us

Flicker @ UJ started with a simple idea — that joy shouldn't be a luxury on campus. Founded by students for students, we're passionate about creating moments that bring people together.

Our events are small in size but big in heart — designed to help you pause, connect, and remember what makes university life special.

Meet Our Founder

Linda Mtshwene

Founder & 2nd Year Student

Linda is a second-year student at UJ who noticed that campus life had become polarized between overwhelming large parties and isolating study sessions. He created Flicker to fill the gap with meaningful, small-scale events that foster genuine connections.

"Flicker started because campus life felt too loud or too lonely. We wanted the space in between — where real connections happen."

Under Linda's leadership, Flicker has grown from a single scavenger hunt event to a thriving community that hosts multiple events each semester, always staying true to the original vision of creating "Small Moments, Big Memories."
